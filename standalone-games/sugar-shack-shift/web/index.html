Sugar Shack Shift

Run a Québec sugar shack for twelve turns. Feed the fire and draw the pan when the syrup gauge hits the golden band.

Draw between 65 and 85. Above 95, the batch scorches.

How to play

Read the next wind before you choose. Adding a log costs one of six logs, raises heat, and concentrates the pan. Simmering uses the remaining heat. Wind cools the pan faster. Drawing uses a turn and refills the pan. Aim for amber at 75 for the best grade. All gauges and timings are game units.
