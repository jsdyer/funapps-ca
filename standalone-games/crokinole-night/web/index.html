Crokinole Night

Pass the board between two players. Line up a disc, flick it past the pegs, and land in the centre hole.

Red to shoot

How to play

House rules: each player gets six discs. Both shoot from the near edge so you can pass one screen. Centre holes bank 20 points. Discs left in the rings score 5, 10 or 15 at the end. You can knock either colour off the board. No opponent-contact foul in this short version.
