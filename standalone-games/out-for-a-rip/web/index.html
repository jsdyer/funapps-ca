How to survive the errand

🚗 Deliver

Follow the big arrow and glowing community-hall beacon. Deliver before “The Weather” runs out.

☕ Boost

Hold Space or the coffee button. It is not medically responsible, but it is quick.

📯 Honk

Honk at gulls, moose, and indecisive RVs. It briefly clears a path.

🛣️ Use roads

Your beater is faster on pavement. PEI players are naturally less alarmed by red dirt.

🪙 Collect empties

Bottle refunds buy permanent upgrades in the Souvenir Shed.

🌦️ Adapt
