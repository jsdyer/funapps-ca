    Field notes & how to play

    The aim: complete the tour with all four postcards and a comfortable ride. Your score rewards photos, comfort, and daylight. Missing a lookoff still lets you finish; replay for a full album.

    Driving: A/D or left/right steer. S/down brakes. W/up accelerates above the gentle cruising speed. P or Escape pauses. At a lookoff, slow below 35 and move onto the right shoulder; press Space or the camera button. Going off the road reduces comfort. Running out of comfort or daylight lets you retry from your most recent photo stop.

    This is a playful, compressed route inspired by Cape Breton, with fictional road geometry and travel times. It is not a driving simulator or navigation guide. Learn about the real Cabot Trail and Cape Breton Highlands National Park.
