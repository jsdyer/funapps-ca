# Boardwalk Bandit

You're a seagull on the Halifax Waterfront boardwalk. Swoop through tourists' fries, fish & chips, ice cream, donairs and lobster rolls before your belly runs empty. Dodge the newspaper swats, out-steal the rival gulls, and cash in when the Noon Gun on Citadel Hill makes everyone drop their snacks.

Play it on FunApps at [/play/boardwalk-bandit/](https://funapps.ca/play/boardwalk-bandit/).

## Controls

- Move the mouse or a finger and the gull follows. Arrow keys or WASD also fly.
- Fly through a snack to grab it. Quick grabs in a row build a combo (up to x5).
- A "!" over a tourist with a rolled-up newspaper means a swat is coming.
- P or Esc pauses.

## Build

No build step. `web/index.html` is a single self-contained file (Canvas 2D, Web Audio, no dependencies) and is copied to `/play/boardwalk-bandit/` by the hub build.
