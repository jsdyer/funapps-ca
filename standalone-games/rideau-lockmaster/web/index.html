Rideau Lockmaster

Work a miniature canal lock. Match the water level on each side, open the gate, and take the boat through.

How to play

A closed chamber can fill or drain by one level each move. At a matching level, open the gate and move the boat. Close the entry gate behind you, match the exit level, and open the exit gate. Later passages descend or begin with water left over from an earlier boat. These are fictional puzzle layouts inspired by the Rideau Canal.
