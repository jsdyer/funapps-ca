THE MAIL MUST GET THROUGH

Today’s little deliveries.

Blocked grid spaces

    Field notes, controls & the real Prairies

    Choose one of eight compass headings and 1–3 sail units. The wind adds one space in its shown direction. A diagonal sail moves that many spaces on both axes. Stop exactly on each delivery marker, then land at the depot. Passing over a stop doesn’t deliver its postcard.

    Wet ground and fences block the whole path, not just the landing. Invalid sails cost nothing. Brake & wait uses one move and changes the wind. Undo takes back a move. The hint suggests a complete route if one remains.

    Mouse and touch use the same controls. Tab and Enter operate every button. Arrow keys choose cardinal headings; Q/E/Z/C choose diagonals; 1/2/3 set sail strength. Space sails when you are not focused on a control. Nothing moves until you choose an action.

    These imaginary courses and grid-based wind rules are a puzzle, not a land-sailing guide. The Maritime perspective is fictional; the big-sky inspiration is real. Explore Saskatchewan’s living skies.

    WELCOME ABOARD THE MAIL BUGGY

    A sailor on
    a sea of grass.

    You’ve come west from the Maritimes, borrowed a little land yacht, and volunteered for the afternoon post.

    1. Read the breeze. It pushes your buggy one extra grid space.
    2. Pick a heading and sail. Check the landing preview before moving.
    3. Make every stop. Deliver the postcards, then reach the depot before your sails run out.

    First-round tip: choose East and Full sail (3). The breeze will carry you right to Golden Elevator.

    No clock to race. Undo and hints are always here.
