How to play Clams & Chips

Run a Maritime fry shack for 90 seconds. Tap a fryer to lower a batch of breaded clam meat or thick-cut chips. Wait until its label says Golden! Lift now, then tap again to lift three cooked portions onto the counter.

Match the quantities on the order ticket, then press Serve order. Serve quickly for a bonus and keep customers happy to build a streak worth up to 3× points. Lifting too early wastes a batch; leave it too long and it burns. Clear a burnt basket before starting again.

Keyboard: 1 for clams, 2 for chips, Space to serve, P or Escape to pause. Sound is optional. Your best score stays in this browser. The game pauses when you leave the tab.
